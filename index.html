Patient voice

I've been thinking about what you said, and about all the work you've already done around website repositioning, market research, strategy and your EOS 3 uniques. I went to your website and also looked at your Google Ads, and thought that mining the patient reviews and objections across maybe a thousand practices near you could give us what we can do more. That's exactly what I did: I pulled the data and surfaced the very real pain points patients are describing right now, in their most recent reviews.

My hope is to build something genuinely useful for you, something that fits what you've already done with your website copy and your ads. Hence, those are the two angles I focused on. Everything here is built from the ground up on real data.

Practices
I analyzed the 15-mile radius across your office, which is around 973 practices. Each listing's rating, review count, star breakdown, categories, services and the reviews Google shows on it were collected on September 15, 2026.
Reviews
Google shows five to seven reviews per listing, which gave 4,393 reviews with text. The quotes in this report were picked from the one- to three-star competitor reviews, all 422 of which I read in full.
Chains
Identified by brand name in the listing title. Offices owned by dental groups under a local name are counted as independent.
Ads
Google's Ads Transparency Center was checked for your website and 30 nearby competitors.

What patients complain about

422 of the competitor reviews I read are one to three stars. 88.2% of them mention money, scheduling, phones or waiting. Only 2.1% are about the dental work alone, with no money or office complaint attached.

Google Ads

Who's advertising near you, and some headlines worth trying

The full list of practices checked, with and without ads, is in Annexure 5.1 and Annexure 5.2.

When someone searches for a dentist near you, most of the ads next to yours belong to the chains, and the chains are where the one-star reviews in Section 3 come from. I wrote the headlines below to answer those complaints head-on. Each one is backed by something already on your site, so the ad and the page it lands on say the same thing.

The price on your estimate is the price you pay.

Answers bills patients did not expect, 60.4% of competitor complaints. Point it at the financial page.

One doctor from the extraction to the crown.

Answers "a different dentist each visit" and new-doctor fees. Builds on your home-page headline.

In pain? Seen within 48 hours by the dentist who will fix it.

Answers emergencies and waiting, 8.5% and 24.2% of competitor complaints. The 48-hour promise is already on your home page.

Afraid of the dentist? IV sedation, here, in one visit.

Answers the fear stories in Section 3. Almost no nearby practice mentions sedation in its reviews or ads.

Full-arch implants with adjustments included.

Answers implant and denture complaints about fit and paying twice. We can run it once we've settled what's included.
